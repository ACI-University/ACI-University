# ACI-University

> **Evidence Before Trust. Research Before Authority. Capital Before Memory.**

ACI-University is building a new infrastructure for **Open, Verifiable, and Reusable Research** — 
where scientific knowledge is not just published, but accumulated, challenged, extended, and inherited.

## 🧱 What is Research Capital?

Research Capital is a tightly packaged unit of verified research output containing:
**Problem · Claim · Evidence · Reasoning · Formalization · Provenance · Verification Status · Limitations**

It transforms scientific contribution from *"the whole building"* to *"verified bricks"* 
that anyone can reuse, fork, challenge, and build upon.

## 🌐 Our Vision

- **GitHub** gave us infrastructure for code.
- **Hugging Face** gave us infrastructure for models and datasets.
- **ACI-University** is building infrastructure for **verified scientific knowledge** — 
  a layer that connects them all into **Civilizational Memory**.
